The goal for the new Trading Strategy Backtester will be:

A complete quantitative trading application that downloads market data, calculates indicators, runs strategies, backtests them realistically, calculates performance metrics, compares against Buy & Hold, exposes everything through FastAPI, displays results in React, and finally adds an AI analysis layer.

Day 10 — First Realistic Backtest

Fix the first major problem you encountered in the old project:

Look-ahead bias.

Don't do:

Signal today
→ Buy today

Instead:

Signal today
     ↓
Buy next day's Open

This is much closer to how a real backtest should operate.

Day 36 — Walk-Forward / Out-of-Sample Testing

This is very important for a finance internship project.

Instead of:

2020 ───────────────── 2025
        Test everything

use:

Training       Testing
2020-2023      2024

Then:

Training       Testing
2021-2024      2025

This helps detect strategies that only work on historical data they were effectively optimized for.

Day 37 — Monte Carlo / Robustness Testing

Take your trades and simulate variations.

For example:

Original trades
      ↓
Randomized trade order
      ↓
1000 simulations
      ↓
Possible portfolio outcomes

Analyze:

Median return
Worst case
Best case
Probability of loss
Maximum drawdown distribution

This makes the project significantly more quantitative.

🔥 PHASE 9 — AI Agent
Days 38–40

This is where your earlier idea of adding an AI agent becomes useful.

The AI should not decide what stock you should buy.

Instead, it should analyze the output of your backtester.

Day 38 — AI Analysis Layer

Create something like:

ai/
    analyzer.py

Input:

{
  "strategy_return": 20.07,
  "cagr": 3.8,
  "sharpe": 0.62,
  "maximum_drawdown": -11.01,
  "win_rate": 60,
  "profit_factor": 1.7
}

AI receives the metrics.

It can explain:

The strategy generated a positive return,
but underperformed Buy & Hold.

The Sharpe ratio indicates moderate
risk-adjusted performance.

The maximum drawdown was relatively
contained at -11%.
